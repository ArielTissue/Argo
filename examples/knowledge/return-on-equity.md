# Return on equity

ROE relates net income to average common equity. Decompose it into profit margin, asset turnover and financial leverage using consistent periods and definitions. An increase driven by debt or a shrinking equity denominator after repurchases differs from operating improvement. Very small or negative equity can make the ratio uninformative. Compare multi-year results, business models and balance-sheet risk rather than promoting one historical threshold to a rule for all industries.
