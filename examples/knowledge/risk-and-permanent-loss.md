# Risk and permanent loss

Consider irreversible impairment, dilution, leverage and forced selling as well as price volatility. A solvent investor may tolerate a temporary quotation decline; funding obligations can make that same decline destructive. Describe the loss mechanism, its plausible severity and the capacity to recover. Correlated exposures can fail together even when individual companies look sound. Low historical volatility is not proof of safety, and a profitable outcome does not establish that the original risk was reasonable.
