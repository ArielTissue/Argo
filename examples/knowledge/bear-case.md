# Bear case

Describe a causal path by which the investment thesis fails, not a generic list of market risks. Include customers, suppliers and financing counterparties when their fragility can damage the company. For each material risk, state the evidence to monitor and the condition that separates temporary difficulty from lasting impairment. A useful bear case can support either continued patience or reassessment as facts arrive. Do not invent probabilities merely to make a scenario look quantitative.
