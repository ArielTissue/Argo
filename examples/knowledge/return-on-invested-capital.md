# Return on invested capital

A common formulation is after-tax operating profit divided by average invested capital. Compare sustainable returns with an explicitly estimated cost of capital and examine incremental reinvestment opportunities. Keep financing effects separate from operating performance. Cash exclusions, goodwill, leases and capitalized intangible investment change the denominator; disclose the convention before comparing sources or companies. A historical spread above capital cost supports further moat research but does not establish future durability or an attractive purchase price.
