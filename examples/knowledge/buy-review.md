# Workflow: evaluate a possible purchase

Input: current research, price timestamp, valuation range, user policy and portfolio snapshot. Compare embedded expectations with conservative scenarios, then examine margin of safety and portfolio capacity independently. Record intended position purpose, funding source, counterargument, review date and invalidation conditions. An attractive standalone valuation may still conflict with portfolio limits. Missing context remains unknown. Output decision support only: Argo validates and stages a report; the user separately decides whether to invest. Explicit file-archive confirmation is not authorization to trade.
