# Concentration

Count exposures rather than ticker symbols alone. Examine single issuers, industries, themes and common economic drivers, including overlaps inside funds and across accounts. Keep estimates and missing look-through data explicit. Research confidence does not eliminate correlated loss. Evaluate a contemplated change using a separately labeled hypothetical post-change portfolio; the current Argo snapshot calculation is not a trade simulator. Position limits and frozen themes must come from the user’s confirmed policy, never from this knowledge note.
