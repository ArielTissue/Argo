# Workflow: review earnings

Input: verified release, reporting period, original statements and notes, prior thesis and unresolved questions. Reconcile year-over-year and sequential comparisons, business drivers, cash flow, capital needs and debt. Distinguish reported figures from adjustments such as SBC sensitivity. Update moat evidence, bear case and valuation scenarios; explain what changed rather than merely restating headlines. Prefer official disclosures and mark unavailable evidence explicitly. Output a sourced analysis with next questions. When the minimum evidence is absent, use insufficient_evidence rather than inventing a valuation.

Related: [[free-cash-flow]] · [[sbc-adjusted-cash-flow]] · [[moat]] · [[bear-case]] · [[buy-review]]

## Report template

1. Release verification, reporting period, units and source locations.
2. Revenue/earnings comparison with reconciled periods; label unavailable prior data.
3. Cash bridge, capital needs, dilution and debt-note changes.
4. Thesis update, bear case, valuation assumptions and unresolved questions with next review date.
