# Workflow: select research candidates

Input: a candidate, research route, business classification and available disclosures. First check disclosure quality, governance, financing dependence and dilution. Use the quick quality screen with explicit exceptions; then test moat durability, cash conversion and the strongest disconfirming explanation. State the potential source of returns and the disagreement with market expectations. Output: reject, watch, or investigate further, with evidence, missing materials and a falsifiable thesis. These research dispositions are narrative guidance, not new CLI verdict enums. A research candidate is not an approved purchase.

Related: [[quick-quality-screen]] · [[moat]] · [[value-traps]] · [[earnings-review]]

## Handoff template

1. Research route and company type; reason the candidate entered the queue.
2. Disclosure check and the most material financing/governance concern.
3. Best supporting evidence, strongest contradiction and why it matters.
4. Disposition with next document and falsifiable question; keep company conclusions outside reusable concept notes.
