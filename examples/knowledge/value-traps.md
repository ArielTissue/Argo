# Value traps

A low multiple can reflect peak-cycle earnings, structural decline, heavy reinvestment, dilution or claims senior to common equity. Identify the denominator behind “cheap” and normalize it where evidence permits. Ask whether the valuation survives without a return to a past business environment. Examine who receives the cash and whether assets can actually be realized. A catalyst may help, but its absence does not itself invalidate a sound cash-generating business; conversely, an exciting catalyst cannot rescue unsupported value.
