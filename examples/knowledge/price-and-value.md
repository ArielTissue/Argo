# Price and value

Business value depends on future distributable cash and uncertainty; market price reflects what buyers currently accept. A compelling industry trend does not establish that today’s price offers an attractive return. Build an independent range, identify the expectations embedded in price, and explain the specific disagreement. Distinguish a good company from an adequately priced claim on that company. Do not infer undervaluation from a fall below a previous quotation or the investor’s purchase price.

Related: [[margin-of-safety]] · [[second-level-thinking]] · [[value-traps]]

## Expectation worksheet

1. Separate operating forecast from market quotation and historical purchase cost.
2. Identify the growth, margin or duration assumption needed to justify the quoted price.
3. Compare that implied assumption with source-backed evidence and an adverse alternative.
4. Record what would close or widen the gap; absence of a near-term catalyst is a timing uncertainty, not proof of mispricing.
