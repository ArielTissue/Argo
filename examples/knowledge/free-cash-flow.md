# Free cash flow

A common starting convention is operating cash flow minus capital expenditure. State the issuer’s exact definition, period, units and source; definitions vary. Reconcile cash conversion, working-capital timing and recurring capital needs before treating the amount as distributable. Positive cash flow can reduce financing dependence, but one strong quarter may simply reverse an earlier timing effect. Growth spending, maintenance requirements, leases and financing arrangements need separate scrutiny. Do not equate every reported FCF measure with owner earnings.
