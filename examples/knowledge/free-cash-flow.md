# Free cash flow

A common starting convention is operating cash flow minus capital expenditure. State the issuer’s exact definition, period, units and source; definitions vary. Reconcile cash conversion, working-capital timing and recurring capital needs before treating the amount as distributable. Positive cash flow can reduce financing dependence, but one strong quarter may simply reverse an earlier timing effect. Growth spending, maintenance requirements, leases and financing arrangements need separate scrutiny. Do not equate every reported FCF measure with owner earnings.

Related: [[sbc-adjusted-cash-flow]] · [[return-on-invested-capital]] · [[cash-flow-quality]]

## Reconcile the number

1. Record operating cash flow from the cash-flow statement, including period, units, currency and whether the statement is cumulative year-to-date.
2. Identify cash expenditure on property, equipment and relevant capitalized investment. State exactly which items the chosen FCF definition deducts.
3. Compute the bridge: operating cash flow minus selected capital expenditure. Reconcile it with the issuer's published FCF and explain any difference.
4. Identify working-capital movements, customer prepayments, taxes, restructuring and other timing effects. A release of working capital may not recur.
5. Examine recurring capital needs, SBC, leases and financing arrangements separately. Do not deduct an item twice simply because it appears in two disclosure tables.

## A historical comparison aid

Dorsey's distilled moat notes use FCF/revenue of at least 5% as one historical signal of cash-generating strength. Treat it as an invitation to investigate sustained cash economics, not as a universal quality line. The same margin means different things in a retailer, a software business and a capital-intensive producer. A business below that level is not automatically poor, and a business above it is not automatically protected from competition.

Compare several periods and peers after reconciling definitions. Distinguish a maintenance-capital estimate from reported total capital spending; management's growth/maintenance split may itself be uncertain. A company investing heavily may have negative current FCF without being worthless, but its funding runway and eventual cash-generation path require evidence.

## Output worksheet

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Source and period | Filing location, date, currency and units |
| Operating cash flow | Reported amount and material timing effects |
| Capital spending | Included line items and exclusions |
| Reported/conventional FCF | Calculation and issuer reconciliation |
| Adjusted sensitivity | Explicit adjustment bridge and rationale |
| Interpretation | Recurring capacity, uncertainty and financing dependence |

Do not label a figure freely distributable while ignoring debt service, minimum operating cash or obligations outside the chosen formula.
