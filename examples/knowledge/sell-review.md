# Workflow: reassess an existing position

Input: the dated original thesis, applicable policy, current evidence and portfolio context. Identify whether the issue is thesis failure, valuation or opportunity cost, predeclared protection, or exposure management. Revisit previously stated bear-case signals and explain whether they have actually occurred. Cost basis and the desire to break even are not sufficient reasons to hold or sell. Record remaining uncertainty and the next review date. The current CLI archives research proposals; it does not implement a sell-order workflow or execute transactions.

Related: [[sell-triggers]] · [[bear-case]] · [[decision-quality]] · [[position-review]]

## Reassessment template

1. Original thesis and timestamped trigger, preserved without retrospective edits.
2. New evidence and whether it supports temporary adversity, thesis failure or uncertainty.
3. Remaining value range, exposure implications and opportunity cost.
4. Narrative judgment and next review date. No claim of an executed sale or an automated sell workflow.
