# Economic moat

Start with evidence of sustained economic returns, then explain the mechanism that protects them from competition. Test switching costs, cost advantages, customer networks, brands and legal barriers against retention, pricing and capital returns. Popular products and fast growth alone are insufficient. Separate the size of excess returns from their likely duration. Patent expiry, substitution and customer bargaining power can shorten that duration. Compare several years and suitable industry peers; historical screening ratios are research prompts rather than timeless cutoffs.
