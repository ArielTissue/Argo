# Quick quality screen

Use a fast screen to decide where to spend research time, not to issue a buy conclusion. Check disclosure availability, recurring operating earnings, cash generation, leverage, earnings stability and unexplained accounting items. Read footnotes before accepting a clean headline. Historical numeric thresholds need industry and cycle context. Small companies, new listings and turnarounds require a deliberately chosen research route; rejecting them automatically would silently combine incompatible screening philosophies. Record the route, exclusions and missing evidence.
